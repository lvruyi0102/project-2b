# Quote / first-invoice template

Use plain language. Do not invent legal advice.

1. Parties (names as they will appear on the transfer)
2. Scope in / scope out (bullet list; out is mandatory)
3. Deliverables and format
4. Timeline (start only after payment or written PO)
5. Price, currency, tax treatment if known
6. Payment method + deadline
7. Revision rounds
8. What happens if the buyer delays materials
9. Confidentiality one-liner
10. Acceptance: silence after N days = accepted or not — pick one and write it

Do not write “guaranteed ROI”.
