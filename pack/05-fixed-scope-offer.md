# Fixed-scope offer (buyer-facing)

This is a draft offer. It is not a signed contract, not a customer, and not revenue.

## What is sold

One paid working session that turns a messy service idea into a sendable offer:

- one-sentence buyer and job-to-be-done
- in-scope / out-of-scope list
- price, deposit, and revision limit
- delivery checklist the seller can attach to an invoice

## Price draft (not collected)

- CNY 1,500 or USD 220, fixed.
- 50% before work, 50% on delivery of the four items above.
- One revision round within 7 days. Extra scope is a new quote.

## What is not included

- company registration, tax filing, payment-account KYC
- guaranteed clients or revenue
- ongoing retainer

## Settlement rule

No payment link is attached. Money is only counted after it settles into the principal's designated legal account.
