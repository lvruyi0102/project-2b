# Positioning one-pager (OPC OS)

For: one-person firms and tiny studios that can deliver work but freeze when writing an offer or issuing a first invoice.

Job to be done: leave the call with a scope, a price band, a yes/no checklist, and a message you can send today.

Not for: people hunting guaranteed riches, regulated medical/investment claims, or anyone who wants a finished company without talking to a buyer.

Promise (non-guarantee): reduce the time from “I can do the work” to “a written offer exists” to under two hours.

Price intent: CNY 99–499 for the pack; CNY 3,980+ only if the buyer wants a custom 48h pipeline built for their offer.
