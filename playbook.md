# OPC OS Playbook v0

From zero cash to a legal first invoice.

## Week 0
Pick one painful, paid workflow. Do not start with “AI for everything”.

## Weeks 1-2
Ship a free artifact that a buyer would screenshot. Measure replies, not vanity traffic.

## Weeks 3-4
Turn the artifact into a paid pack: templates, checklists, prompts, review rubric.
Price in the range a solo operator can expense without a committee.

## Weeks 5-8
If 10+ people pay, wrap the pack into a thin product (workspace / generator / tracker).
If fewer than 10 pay, kill the niche and rotate.

## Weeks 9-12
Reinvest 70% of net cash into distribution that you can measure weekly.
Do not hire. Do not raise. Do not declare victory on followers.

## Hard rules
- No fabricated revenue
- No platform ToS evasion
- No claiming settlement until money is in the designated account
