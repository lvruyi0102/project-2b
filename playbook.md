# OPC OS Playbook v0.1

From zero cash to a legal first invoice. Then only keep what buyers pay for.

## Constraint

Starting cash is zero. Do not spend a growth budget you do not have. Do not hire. Do not raise to feel progress.

## Week 0
Pick one painful, already-paid workflow. Current pick: first commercial offer + first invoice for a one-person firm.

## Weeks 1-2
Ship a free artifact a buyer would screenshot.
Current artifacts: this playbook + public landing.
Measure replies and "I would pay if" sentences, not page views.

## Weeks 3-4
Paid pack: templates, checklists, prompt library, review rubric.
Price: an amount a solo operator can expense without a committee (target band CNY 99–499 or USD 19–49).
Do not open payment until the pack is real files, not an outline.

## Weeks 5-8
If ≥10 people pay: wrap into a thin product (generator / tracker).
If <10 pay: kill the niche and rotate. Sunk time is not a reason to continue.

## Weeks 9-12
Reinvest ~70% of net cash into one measurable distribution channel.
Do not declare success on followers, stars, or media.

## Hard rules
- No fabricated revenue, users, contracts, or balances
- No platform ToS evasion
- No claiming settlement until money is in the designated account
- 2亿 is the terminal goal, not a Phase-1 KPI
