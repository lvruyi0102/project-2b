# Phase 1 decision (2026-09-20)

## What the target actually requires

CNY 200,000,000 settled net is not a content-hobby outcome and not a single template pack outcome.

Realistic compounding sequence from CNY 0:

1. First legal invoice (proof of a buyer).
2. Repeatable offer with measurable conversion.
3. Reinvest into distribution and a thin product.
4. Only after product-market fit: raise ACV (productized service) or raise volume (SaaS).
5. 2亿 is only in scope if a product or service becomes a real company with distribution. That is years, not a sprint. Declaring otherwise would be fiction.

## Paths considered and ranking for *this* operator

Operator constraints: no starting cash; AI can write, code, deploy drafts; cannot complete KYC, open merchant accounts, sign as legal person, or move bank funds.

| Path | Why considered | Verdict now |
|---|---|---|
| Novel / IP first | Low cash start | Rejected as primary. Slow cash, winner-take-most, weak control of distribution. May return later as a channel, not as the engine. |
| Generic AI wrapper SaaS | Fast to code | Rejected as first bet. Crowded, paid acquisition needed, churn high. |
| China knowledge community first | Local payment familiarity | Weak without an existing audience. Building audience from zero delays first invoice. |
| Funded startup / valuation | Large headline numbers exist in AI | Rejected. Valuation is not net income. Also requires founders, equity, and legal entities. |
| Productized service + artifact | High ACV, can start with documents | **Selected.** Fastest honest path to a real buyer, then productize the workflow. |
| Vertical AI tool after 10 paying users | Higher ceiling | Deferred until paid demand is observed. |

## Selected path

**OPC OS: one painful paid workflow → free artifact → paid pack → thin generator if ≥10 buyers.**

First workflow chosen: *independent operator offer + first-invoice system* (positioning, scope, price, contract checklist, delivery checklist). Reason: the buyer is the same person we can reach in public text; the artifact can be given away; the paid pack does not require a large engineering surface.

## Kill criteria (auto-rotate)

- 30 days of public distribution and <20 serious replies → change niche or channel.
- Paid pack live 30 days and <10 payments → kill pack, keep only what converted.
- Any legal/compliance ambiguity → stop that SKU immediately.

## What is *not* done

- No revenue.
- No customers.
- No payment provider.
- No domain pointed for commerce.
- No claim that 2亿 is probable on a fixed timetable.

## Blockers that require the human principal

Only when we have something worth selling:

- Merchant account / 收款账户 (Stripe / Lemon Squeezy / 爱发电 / 微信收付款 as legally applicable)
- Identity verification
- Designated settlement account confirmation

Until then, work stays on artifacts, distribution copy, and product drafts.
